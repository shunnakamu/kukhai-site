Electra — AI algorithm platform for cardiac ablation

Less repetitive work. More focus on clinical decisions.

Electra is designed to run inside the EP workstation, measuring, labeling and selecting intracardiac signals in real time — with results linked back to the original signals. The physician makes the call.

Talk to us→

Complex ablation runs on manual signal analysis

Much of the interval measurement, activation matching and channel review is still done by hand. In an illustrative four-hour complex case, about two hours go to diagnosis and manual analysis.

We heard similar challenges from U.S. EP specialists in 2026. Atrial fibrillation recurs after ablation in 20–40% of patients.1

1 Mayo Clinic, June 2025; recurrence varies by population and follow-up. Time allocation is illustrative, not measured workflow data.

Inside your system, not a replacement for it

Electra is designed to read the signals the lab already records and return results to the workstation's own display, in real time.
